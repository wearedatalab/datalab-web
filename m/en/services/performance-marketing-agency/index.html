Quick answer

What does a performance marketing agency like DataLab do?

DataLab is a performance marketing agency that plans, launches and optimizes paid campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon Ads and DV360. Every campaign is tied to conversion events in GA4; audiences, bids and ads are adjusted weekly; and results are reported as cost per lead, cost per acquisition and ROAS.

The problem

Problems we solve

✕ 01

Paying for clicks, not customers

Without conversion events wired in, platforms optimize for cheap traffic. The report looks good; revenue doesn't move.

✕ 02

Keywords that attract the wrong buyer

Without a strict negative keyword list, you pay for job seekers, complaints and products you don't sell. That click costs you twice.

✕ 03

One budget for everything

When every product shares a campaign, you can't tell which one performs or where the next dollar should go.

✕ 04

Reports without decisions

Reach and impressions every month, but nobody says what to pause, what to scale or why.

What's included

What a Google Ads agency should measure, and what to demand from yours

A Google Ads agency shouldn't be judged by how many ads it launches, but by what each customer costs. That's why the first thing we do isn't turning campaigns on. It's wiring up measurement: GA4, Google Tag Manager and conversion events (lead, quote, WhatsApp click or purchase), broken out by product and channel.

With that foundation, paid media gets optimized for what matters. On Google we compete for buying-intent searches and block the ones that bring the wrong buyer. On Meta and TikTok we build lookalike audiences from people who already bought, not people who clicked. Every week, budget moves toward the audiences and ads that are already working.

Ask any PPC agency for three things: full access to your accounts, a dashboard that shows cost per sale, and a clear explanation of what was paused and why.

How our PPC agency picks the channel: Google Ads, Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn

Channels do different jobs. Google Ads captures existing demand: someone searching for an auto insurance quote has already decided they need one. Meta and TikTok create demand, reaching people by age, location, interests and life stage before they search. LinkedIn Ads reaches decision-makers by title, industry and company size. Amazon Ads serves brands selling on the marketplace, and DV360 delivers cross-media reach for large campaigns.

The media plan defines each channel's role in the funnel and assigns budget by product or line, not one lump sum, so you know what deserves more next month. If you're not sure yet who to target or with what budget, start with digital marketing consulting. If you're a US company selling into Latin America, a nearshore paid media agency in Colombia adds regional market experience, and Bogotá's clock (UTC−5, no daylight saving time) keeps us within an hour of New York all year and two to three hours ahead of California.

Frequently asked questions

Performance marketing: frequently asked questions

01

How much does a PPC agency charge?

At DataLab, paid media management is a monthly fee set by tiers of your total media spend. Media spend is separate, and you pay it directly on each platform. Ad creative, landing pages or CRM integration are quoted separately if you need them. The detailed proposal is free of charge.

02

How much should I spend on ads to start?

There's no single number: it depends on your product, your market, the cost per click in your category and your lead or sales target. We recommend starting with a contained budget to test audiences, messages and formats, then increasing spend in phases only when the data shows what works.

03

How long until campaigns show results?

Campaigns go live in week 4, and the first 15 days of each campaign are a learning period when platforms underperform. Results typically start showing within 6 to 8 weeks, depending on how mature your channels are. For measurable, stable results we recommend a minimum of six months.

04

Who pays for media and who owns the accounts?

You do. Media is paid with your card directly on Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn or Amazon, and the accounts, data and creative belong to your company. DataLab works with admin access, signs a non-disclosure agreement and handles information under Colombia's personal data protection law (Law 1581 of 2012).

05

Which ad platforms do you manage?

We manage Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Amazon Ads and Display & Video 360 (DV360). When a campaign calls for it, we also plan complementary media, like the travel metasearch sites, TV and radio combined in Avianca's Red Sale campaign.

06

What is ROAS and how do you measure it?

ROAS (return on ad spend) divides the revenue attributed to paid media by the media investment. To measure it properly we connect GA4, the ad pixels and your CRM or store, and record every sale with its source campaign. Alongside ROAS we report cost per lead and cost per acquisition in a live dashboard.

07

What if I already have campaigns running?

We start with an audit of your accounts: structure, conversions, keywords, audiences and creative. We don't shut down what works. We fix tracking, prune keywords and audiences that spend without converting, and reorganize budget by product, without pausing the campaigns that already deliver results.

08

Can you manage paid media in multiple countries?

Yes. We ran Rubbermaid's paid media across Latin America for five years and handled media planning for Avianca's Red Sale campaign in 18 countries. DataLab is headquartered in Bogotá, with a presence in Mexico, Peru, Chile and the United States. We usually validate one market first, then scale to the rest with what we learned.
